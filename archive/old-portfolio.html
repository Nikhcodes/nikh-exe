01 · Field report

A little about
the operator

I spend my days in IT operations at a fintech banking environment, and my nights building software. The two jobs keep arguing with each other, productively.

Operations taught me to care about what happens after the deploy: how access is controlled, what a system does when something breaks, and who finds out first. I'm now studying BSc IT / Software Engineering at UNASAT and steering toward backend engineering.

That means Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, authentication, application security and failure handling. I've also built full-stack products and still enjoy shipping a whole thing end to end. I'm early in my career and I'm learning in public, one system at a time.

05 · Notes from the cockpit

How I think

Auth is not a login form

Sessions, token lifetimes, hashing and who may do what once inside. The form is the smallest part.

Failure modes first

State transitions and failure paths decide whether a system is trustworthy. The happy path mostly takes care of itself.

Operations shaped me

Watching real systems run taught me to ask what happens at 2 a.m. when this breaks.

I don't collect frameworks. I'd rather understand a system properly than skim ten tutorials.
